# The Lens Check illustration prompts

Generated one at a time using the built-in image generation tool.

Final assets: `public/images/lens-check/`.
Public URL prefix: `/images/lens-check/`.
All seven deliverables are opaque 1024 × 1024 PNGs. The tool produced 1254 × 1254 originals; final files were exported at the requested dimensions using bicubic resampling. The first generated image was the style reference for the remaining six. No website components were changed.

```text
Generate ONE finished square 1024 x 1024 PNG illustration.

Style: soft watercolour and delicate ink wash, gentle mist, calm and literary, a quality illustrated book. Restrained translucent pigment, subtle organic wash blooms, sparse charcoal fine details, soft disappearing edges. Generous negative space.
Palette ONLY: plain warm cream background #F5F0EA; forest and sage greens #3D5948, #4E6E5B, #8FA294; fine charcoal #2A2722; a single soft pale gold accent #D9B98A for the small sun. All lighter values are dilute pigment on cream. No blue, red, orange, purple, bright yellow or pure white.
Composition: centred landscape vignette, all meaningful details comfortably within a circle of diameter 80% of the canvas centred at (512,512), suitable for circular cropping. Keep the figure, sun and bends in the path within this safe area. The scenery dissolves softly into the cream with ample breathing room; outermost 10% on each edge is plain uniform #F5F0EA right to every edge. Do not draw a circle or crop the square. No hard horizon spanning the canvas.
No text, lettering, logos, signatures, watermarks, frames, borders or faces. Opaque cream background.
```
