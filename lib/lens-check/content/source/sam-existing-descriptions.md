**The Reluctant Hero**

*You are someone with far more to offer than you are currently letting the world see.*

You are capable, perceptive and exceptional at the things that matter. The people closest to you already know this. You have a depth of understanding, a quality of care and a natural intelligence that surfaces whenever you allow it to. When you step fully into something and give it your all, you are genuinely impressive.

The tension is that something keeps you one step back from fully committing to your own potential. It isn’t laziness or lack of motivation although from the outside looking in it looks a lot like this. It is more of a sense that the moment is not quite right, that you are not quite ready, that someone might notice and then expect more from you in an area you might no longer want to pursue. You are not in the wings because you lack ability. You are there because stepping onto the stage feels like a risk that has not yet felt worth taking.

*The ability has always been there. What changes next is the decision to stop waiting for the right moment and create it by truly finding who you are, what a path that is your own looks like and then commit to walking it.*



**The Wandering Scholar**

You are someone with a genuine curiosity about life that most people lose very early on.

You are open, exploratory and genuinely interested in the world around you. You ask questions others do not think to ask and you have a quality of aliveness to you that comes from never quite settling into the comfortable and the predictable. This can be misconstrued as difficult or challenging as you struggle to silence this innate desire to ask “Why?” and constantly dive below the surface so many people are happy to bob along on. You have seen more, thought more and experienced more than many people and it shows in the way you engage with things.

The tension is that the open road is brilliant for discovery and difficult for arrival. You are good at moving and good at exploring but the question of where you actually want to land, what you want to build and who you want to be when the movement stops has not yet been fully answered. Movement and direction are not the same thing and occasionally the freedom of one is being used to avoid the commitment of the other.

*This is not about stopping or silencing that beautiful mind but instead turning those questions inward and through self understanding, discovering which direction you want to be heading.*

**The Loyal Guard**

*You are someone who shows up for the people in your life in a way that is becoming exceedingly more rare.*

You are dependable, generous and the kind of person others feel safe around. You hold things together for others and being a supportive peer comes naturally to you. You remember what matters to people and you show up for them without needing to be asked. The relationships in your life are better because you are in them and the people who know you well feel that deeply.

The tension is that loyal guards are so focused on protecting and providing for everyone inside the walls that they rarely ask what they themselves need. You give freely and without conditions to the tribe around you but there is a version of generosity that costs more than it should and leaves the person giving it depleted in ways they have learned not to mention.

You are far too willing to make sure everyone else has a full bucket even if it means you are giving the last scraps from your own to make this happen.

*The question worth sitting with is not how to give less. It is how to make sure that some of what you give so generously to others is also finding its way back to you.*



**The Alchemist**

*You are someone with an unusual ability to make sense of things and piece together social puzzles others are left confused by.*

You are perceptive, articulate and you have a gift for seeing patterns and meaning where others see noise. You understand people quickly and intuitively. You can take something complex and make it clear and that ability has served you and the people around you extraordinarily well. There is a quality to the way your mind works that is unique and necessary to deal with complex social issues.

The tension is that alchemists spend so much time translating the world for others that they can neglect the work of understanding themselves. You are brilliant at decoding what is happening around you and considerably less practised at turning that same attention inward with the same honesty and precision. You have the tools but have just not fully applied them to yourself yet.

*The most important thing you have not yet translated is the gap between the life you are living and the one you actually want.*



**The Sleeping Giant**

*You are someone with a capacity that has not yet been fully met by the life you are living.*

There is a depth, an energy and a potential in you that surfaces in flashes and then gets pushed back down. The people closest to you have seen it and know what the fullest version of you is truly capable of. You have felt it yourself in the moments when everything aligns and you operate at a level that reminds you what is possible if you do not hold yourself back. Those moments are previews into the giant within waking up and taking the steering wheel.

The tension is that something keeps the full version of you contained. It isn’t circumstance or lack of ability but rather a reluctance to fully commit to your own potential in case it asks more of you than feels manageable, or draws a level of attention that feels uncomfortable. The better version of you is waiting in the room next door for you to finally give it permission to live on full speed. It is only you that can drop the guard and give it that permission.

*The capacity has always been there. What changes next is the decision to stop treating it as something for later.*



**The Lighthouse**

*You are someone that other people navigate by without fully realising it.*

You are steady, reliable and there is a trustiness that radiates from within that people feel when they are around you. You are the one others come to when they need to think something through, when they need someone to hold the line or when they simply need to feel less alone. That is not common, it is something that reflects a real calm and sturdiness in you that others find comfort in.

The tension is that lighthouses stay in one place. They guide everyone else to where they are going while remaining fixed at the point they have always occupied. You are brilliant at being a constant for the people around you and considerably less practised at asking where you yourself want to go. Your direction has sometimes been determined by where you are most needed rather than where you most want to be.

*You have been the fixed point for a long time. It is worth asking what it would look like to move.*



**The Storm Watcher**

*You are someone with a sharp and honest relationship with reality that most people lack the courage to develop.*

You see things clearly including the difficult things and you do not pretend otherwise. There is an honesty to how you engage with life that you find more and more rare in others. You are not someone who gets caught off guard because you have already considered most of what could go wrong. That awareness has protected you more than once and the people around you more than they probably realise.

The tension is that the same mind that protects you from disappointment can also protect you from possibility. When you are very good at spotting what could go wrong it becomes difficult to fully commit to what could go right. Over time the space between where you are and where you actually want to be slowly widens without you quite noticing it happening.

*The clarity you have about what could go wrong is useful. The work is learning to apply that same sharp intelligence to what could go extraordinarily well and to develop the faith that you can deal with those hurdles you foresee as they come.*



**The Method Actor**



*You are someone who has become exceptionally skilled at showing up in the way each situation requires.*

You are adaptable, socially intelligent and have an ability to be able to read a room faster than almost anyone. You know what people need from you and you have mastered the art of delivering that seamlessly. In any environment you find a way to fit, to connect and to be received well. That is a skill that has opened doors and built relationships that really matter to you and so you have harnessed it more and more.

The tension is that when you are this good at being what each situation needs it becomes very difficult to locate what you actually are when nothing is needed from you. The performance is so practised and so natural that the person underneath it has not been asked to fully show up for a while and in turn has drifted away. Meaning that when the crowd disperses you are left feeling lost.

*The mask has never been the problem but rather forgetting that you are wearing one is. You must stop being in a constant state of adaptation to finally find who your true self is.*



**The Heir**

*You are someone who has taken what was given to you and made something impressive from it.*

You are responsible, considered and you have honoured the expectations around you in ways that have had huge positive effects in your life. You work hard, you show up and the life you have built reflects a great deal of genuine effort and commitment. There is real substance to what you have created even though initially you had to adapt to the materials you were handed.

The tension is that the blueprint you have been following was drawn up before you had any say in the design. The career, the direction, the version of success you have been working towards may have been handed to you before you were old enough to choose it for yourself. You have caught plenty of carrots you were told that you were supposed to be chasing. The question worth sitting with is whether you would have even chased those particular things if you had decided what is important to you in the first place.

*This is not about tearing anything down. It is about asking, perhaps for the first time, what you would build if the choice were entirely yours. If nobody could judge you.*



**The Observer**

*You are someone with an exceptional ability to understand what is happening beneath the surface of things.*

You are perceptive, intelligent and you see through situations quickly. You pick up on things other people miss and you have a way of making sense of complexity that most people find hard to comprehend. You are rarely fooled and rarely caught off guard as your curiosity often sniffs out most things before they are able to catch you off guard. our ability to read between the lines is one of your most valuable qualities.

The tension is that observers spend most of their time examining everything around them and relatively little time turning that same sharp attention inward. You can diagnose a situation with remarkable precision and you are considerably less practised at applying that same scrutiny to your own patterns, your own motivations and the gap between who you are presenting yourself as and who you actually are. In other words you have spent so much time looking out and mastering the art of analysing that you have never truly took the time to analyse what is inside yourself.

*You already have the tools, you just have not yet fully turned them on yourself yet.*



**The Undercurrent**

*You are someone whose impact on the world around you is more significant than you typically allow yourself to acknowledge.*

You are principled, determined and you have a way of moving through the world that shifts things without making a big deal about it. You care deeply about righteous things and that caring is true and rather than orchestrated or over performed for others. When you decide something matters you are extraordinarily committed to it and the people who have seen you fully engaged with something rarely forget it and even take inspiration from your passionate work ethic.

The tension is that revolutionaries often hold back the full force of what they think and feel in order to keep things smooth. You have strong views, deep convictions and a clarity about what is right that you do not always fully express. Something holds you back from the full volume of yourself and over time that holding back has racked up a cost that is easy to underestimate.

*You have been self-silencing and keeping quiet for a long time but now it is worth asking what would happen if you allowed your unapologetic self to take control.*



**The Navigator**

*You are someone who understands the terrain of life better than most.*

You are observant, thoughtful and perceptive. You notice things other people walk straight past. You have a natural ability to read situations, understand people and make sense of complexity. People often gravitate towards you because you seem to have a clarity about things that they lack and you rarely disappoint when someone needs a clear head in a complicated moment.

The tension is that you spend so much time mapping other people’s worlds that you have not yet fully explored your own. You understand the landscape but you have not yet decided which direction you actually want to travel in. You have the tools and the intelligence. You just have not yet drawn your own map yet.

*The next step is not learning more about the world. It is deciding what you actually want from it and then building a route that gets you there.*
